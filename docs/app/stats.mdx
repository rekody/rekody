---
title: "Stats"
description: "Home shows what your voice has been doing: words dictated and time saved."
---

The Home screen keeps a running picture of your dictation:

- **Words dictated.** The total that has flowed through Rekody.
- **Time saved.** Speaking is faster than typing; this is the difference, added up.

The numbers are computed from your local history, on your Mac. Like everything else in Rekody there is no server tallying your usage, so the stats are for you, not for us.

<Note>
  Deleting entries from your [Library](/app/library) is always available, and your stats are only ever derived from what is stored locally.
</Note>
