---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "The four things that most often go wrong, and how to fix each one."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The text did not get typed">
    Rekody needs your system's permission to place text where your cursor is. Open System Settings, find Rekody under Privacy and Security, and make sure every permission it asked for during setup is still on. Accessibility is the one that lets Rekody type for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rekody will not record">
    Check that microphone access is on for Rekody in System Settings. The app shows a reminder if it is off. Once microphone access is granted, dictation starts immediately, with the pill's waveform confirming that Rekody hears you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dictation is disabled in a password field">
    That is on purpose. Rekody turns itself off in password and other secure fields, so your secrets are never transcribed or stored in your history. Switch to a normal text field and it comes back.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The first dictation seems stuck">
    That is the one-time model download finishing. Keep Rekody open and stay on a network. Your words are captured while it loads, so nothing you say is lost. Every dictation after this one runs fully offline.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
